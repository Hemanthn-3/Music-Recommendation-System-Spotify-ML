Audio Feature Vector Space

Understand how our unsupervised k-NN model computes distance in Spotify's audio dimension matrix

14 Audio Dimensions

Each track is mapped into a 14-dimensional continuous space and normalized with StandardScaler to eliminate scale biases.

  • Acousticness: Confidence measure of whether the track is acoustic (0.0 to 1.0).
  • Danceability: How suitable a track is for dancing based on tempo, rhythm stability, beat strength.
  • Energy: Perceptual measure of intensity, activity, dynamic range, and timbre.
  • Valence: Musical positiveness. High valence sounds happy/cheerful, low sounds sad/angry.
  • Instrumentalness: Predicts whether a track contains no vocals.
  • Liveness: Detects presence of an audience in the recording.
  • Speechiness: Detects presence of spoken words.
  • Tempo: Overall estimated tempo of a track in beats per minute (BPM).
  • Loudness: Overall loudness in decibels (dB).
  • Popularity: Spotify popularity index (0 to 100).

Algorithm: Cosine Proximity

Similarity(u, v) = 1 - CosineDistance(u, v) = (u • v) / (||u|| ||v||)

By computing brute-force Cosine Metric over 157,519 standardized feature rows, query execution resolves in sub-15ms with zero distortion.
